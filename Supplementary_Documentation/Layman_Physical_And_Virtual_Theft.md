# Prevent physical and virtual theft

What experts say: A real HSM protects against attackers who physically probe the chip, or who break out of a virtual machine to steal keys from the host.

Layperson explanation:

Imagine you keep your bank PIN written on a piece of paper.
- Virtual theft: You store it on a piece of paper inside a cardboard box. An intruder breaks into the room and just takes the paper.
- Physical theft: The paper is inside a fireproof safe. The intruder brings a welding torch, cuts the safe open, and takes it.

A real hardware HSM is designed so that even if someone cuts it open with expensive lab equipment, the keys self‑destruct or are impossible to read.
